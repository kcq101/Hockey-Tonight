Stick
Touch anywhere on the left to skate.
Buttons
Four buttons in a diamond. Top: Pass / Switch player. Left: Slapshot / Poke check. Right: Wrist shot / Body check. Bottom: Speed.
Speed
Hold the bottom button for a burst. The green ring is your stamina; it refills when you let go.
Pass
Tap the top button to pass where you're skating, or swipe off it toward a teammate. You can also tap a teammate on the ice.
Shots
Right button is a quick wrist shot. Left button winds up a slapshot: hold to power it up, let go to fire. Swipe off either to pick your corner.
One-timer
Press either shot button while a pass is on its way to you (they glow). Cross-ice one-timers are the hardest for goalies.
Spin-o-rama
While carrying, circle the stick all the way around. Shoot right after and the goalie has bitten on the deke.
Wraparound
Carry the puck behind the net and come out the other side. Shoot right away to stuff it in at the post.
Defense
Bottom and right buttons are a speed burst, and turn into a body check when an opponent is close (they turn red and say Check). Left button pokes the puck loose with your stick. Top button switches player.
Keys
WASD or arrows. J: top. U: left. K or Space: right. L or Shift: bottom. P pause.
